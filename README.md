# 🚨 Emergency Light Cum Advertising Display Board

A PCB-based **Emergency Light Cum Advertising Display Board** developed as a **Second Year Electronics & Computer Engineering Mini Project** under **Dr. Babasaheb Ambedkar Technological University (DBATU)**.

This project automatically switches to battery-powered emergency lighting during a power failure while also functioning as an LED advertising display under normal conditions.

---

## 📌 Project Overview

The Emergency Light Cum Advertising Display Board is designed to provide emergency illumination whenever the main AC supply fails. During normal operation, the LED board can be used for advertising or display purposes. The circuit is implemented on a custom-designed PCB using commonly available electronic components.

## ⚙️ Working Principle

1. Under normal AC supply, the battery is charged.
2. The LED display works as an advertising board.
3. When the AC supply fails, the relay switches automatically.
4. The battery powers the emergency LEDs.
5. Once power returns, the system switches back to normal operation and resumes battery charging.
